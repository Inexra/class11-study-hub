Chapter 2: Motion in a Straight Line

Original chapter-specific practice paper

Maximum Marks: 40Suggested time: 60 minutes
Instructions: Answer all 20 questions. Marks are shown in brackets. Show calculation steps and units. Original practice questions, not an official examination paper.

Section A · 1-mark questions — 8 × 1 = 8 marks

  1. Define instantaneous velocity.[1]
  2. Slope of an x-t graph?[1]
  3. Acceleration at constant velocity?[1]
  4. Can speed be negative?[1]
  5. SI unit of acceleration?[1]
  6. Runner returns to start: net displacement?[1]
  7. Equation linking v,u,a,t?[1]
  8. Area under v-t graph?[1]

Section B · 2-mark questions — 6 × 2 = 12 marks

  1. 150 m in 30 s: average speed?[2]
  2. Velocity 4 to 16 m/s in 6 s: acceleration?[2]
  3. 10 m/s car brakes at 2 m/s²: stopping time?[2]
  4. Convert 90 km/h to m/s.[2]
  5. Move 8 m east then 3 m west: distance/displacement?[2]
  6. From rest at 3 m/s² for 4 s: final speed?[2]

Section C · 3-mark questions — 4 × 3 = 12 marks

  1. u=6 m/s, a=2 m/s², t=5 s: find v,s.[3]
  2. Train at 20 m/s stops in 5 s: a and distance?[3]
  3. Velocity rises 2 to 14 m/s in 6 s: a and displacement?[3]
  4. u=12 m/s, a=1.5 m/s² for 8 s: v,s?[3]

Section D · 4-mark questions — 2 × 4 = 8 marks

  1. Car at 25 m/s brakes at 5 m/s²: stop time and distance?[4]
  2. x=4+3t−t²: v(t), a and time at rest?[4]

End of paper — review signs, units and working.
