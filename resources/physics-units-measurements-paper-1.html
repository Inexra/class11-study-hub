STUDYNEST · CLASS 11 PHYSICS

QUESTION PAPER 1

Chapter 1: Units and Measurements

Practice paper · Original questions

Maximum Marks: 40Suggested Time: 60 minutes
General instructions
1. Answer all questions. 2. Marks for each question are shown in brackets. 3. Use SI units unless stated otherwise. 4. Show working for numerical questions. 5. This is a practice paper, not an official examination paper.

Section A — 1 mark each (8 × 1 = 8 marks)

  1. Write the SI unit of luminous intensity. [1]
  2. How many significant figures are present in 0.00450? [1]
  3. Write the dimensional formula of velocity. [1]
  4. What is the SI unit of the universal gravitational constant? [1]
  5. Is plane angle a dimensionless quantity? [1]
  6. Write the SI prefix symbol for micro. [1]
  7. State one limitation of dimensional analysis. [1]
  8. Express one nanometre in metres. [1]

Section B — 2 marks each (6 × 2 = 12 marks)

  1. Convert 90 km h⁻¹ into m s⁻¹. [2]
  2. Find the dimensional formula of pressure. [2]
  3. Distinguish between accuracy and precision. [2]
  4. Round 0.006784 to three significant figures. [2]
  5. A length and breadth are measured as (5.2 ± 0.1) cm and (3.1 ± 0.1) cm. Find the maximum absolute error in their sum. [2]
  6. Write any two SI base quantities with their units. [2]

Section C — 3 marks each (4 × 3 = 12 marks)

  1. Using dimensional analysis, check whether the equation v² = u² + 2as is dimensionally consistent. [3]
  2. The length, breadth and height of a cuboid are 2.0 cm, 3.0 cm and 4.0 cm. Calculate its volume and state the result to the appropriate number of significant figures. [3]
  3. A physical quantity Q is given by Q = A²B / C³. If the percentage errors in A, B and C are 1%, 2% and 1%, respectively, estimate the maximum percentage error in Q. [3]
  4. Write the dimensional formulae of (i) force, (ii) work, and (iii) power. [3]

Section D — 4 marks each (2 × 4 = 8 marks)

  1. The period T of a simple pendulum depends on its length l and acceleration due to gravity g. Using dimensional analysis, obtain a relation of the form T ∝ lᵃgᵇ. [4]
  2. A student measures a rectangular sheet as length (12.4 ± 0.1) cm and breadth (8.2 ± 0.1) cm. Calculate (i) its area, (ii) the maximum fractional error in the area, and (iii) the maximum percentage error. [4]

End of question paper · Check your calculations and units before submitting.
