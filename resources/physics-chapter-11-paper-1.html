Original chapter-specific practice paper

Maximum Marks: 40Suggested time: 60 minutes
Instructions: Answer all 20 questions. Marks are shown in brackets. Show calculation steps and units. These are original practice questions, not an official examination paper.

Section A · 1-mark questions — 8 × 1 = 8 marks

  1. State the zeroth law.[1]
  2. Define internal energy.[1]
  3. First law with W done by system?[1]
  4. Meaning of adiabatic?[1]
  5. Meaning of isothermal?[1]
  6. Can a cyclic heat engine be 100% efficient?[1]
  7. Ideal gas equation?[1]
  8. Carnot efficiency?[1]

Section B · 2-mark questions — 6 × 2 = 12 marks

  1. At constant volume, 500 J heat added: work?[2]
  2. Gas absorbs 800 J and does 300 J work: ΔU?[2]
  3. Carnot T_h=600K,T_c=300K: efficiency?[2]
  4. Ideal-gas isothermal process: ΔU?[2]
  5. 1 mol with C_v=20 J/mol K and ΔT=5 K: ΔU?[2]
  6. Isothermal versus adiabatic?[2]

Section C · 3-mark questions — 4 × 3 = 12 marks

  1. Gas absorbs 1000 J and does 400 J work: ΔU?[3]
  2. Carnot T_h=500K,T_c=300K: η?[3]
  3. Isobaric gas expands .01 to .03 m³ at 100 kPa. Work?[3]
  4. Why no real cyclic engine converts all heat to work?[3]

Section D · 4-mark questions — 2 × 4 = 8 marks

  1. Explain reversible and irreversible processes with examples.[4]
  2. Engine absorbs 1500 J from 600K, rejects to 300K; actual work=500J. Find Carnot limit, actual efficiency and Qout.[4]
