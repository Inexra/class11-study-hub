QUESTION PAPER 1

Chapter 11: Thermodynamics

Original practice paper

Maximum Marks: 40Suggested Time: 60 minutes
Instructions: Answer all questions. Marks are shown in brackets. Show working for numerical/derivation questions. This is an original practice paper, not an official examination paper.

Section A — 1-mark questions (8 × 1 = 8 marks)

  1. Define Thermal equilibrium. [1]
  2. State one law or principle related to Zeroth law of thermodynamics. [1]
  3. Write a formula associated with Heat, internal energy and work. [1]
  4. Name the SI unit of the principal quantity in First law of thermodynamics. [1]
  5. What is meant by Specific heat capacity? [1]
  6. Give one application of Zeroth law of thermodynamics. [1]
  7. State one assumption used when studying Heat, internal energy and work. [1]
  8. Mention one factor that affects First law of thermodynamics. [1]

Section B — 2-mark questions (6 × 2 = 12 marks)

  1. Explain Thermal equilibrium briefly with an example. [2]
  2. Write the governing relation for Zeroth law of thermodynamics and define its symbols. [2]
  3. Distinguish between two related concepts involved in Heat, internal energy and work. [2]
  4. A relevant quantity changes from 4 units to 10 units. Find the change and percentage increase in a context involving First law of thermodynamics. [2]
  5. State two features or assumptions of Specific heat capacity. [2]
  6. Explain one way Thermodynamic state variables and equation of state is used to solve a physical problem. [2]

Section C — 3-mark questions (4 × 3 = 12 marks)

  1. Explain the principle behind Thermal equilibrium and state its key equation. [3]
  2. For a system involving Zeroth law of thermodynamics, identify the quantities, write the equation and explain the solution steps. [3]
  3. Compare two cases of Heat, internal energy and work and explain how their physical behaviour differs. [3]
  4. Describe an application of First law of thermodynamics, state the relevant law and explain how it is used quantitatively. [3]

Section D — 4-mark questions (2 × 4 = 8 marks)

  1. Starting from the relevant physical law, derive the principal equation used for Specific heat capacity. Define the symbols and state the conditions for validity. [4]
  2. Solve an application problem involving Thermodynamic state variables and equation of state. State assumptions, write the governing equations, show the method and include an appropriate unit in the final answer. [4]
