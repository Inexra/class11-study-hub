Chapter 9: Mechanical Properties of Fluids

Original chapter-specific practice paper

Maximum Marks: 40Suggested time: 60 minutes
Instructions: Answer all 20 questions. Marks are shown in brackets. Show calculation steps and units. These are original practice questions, not an official examination paper.

Section A · 1-mark questions — 8 × 1 = 8 marks

  1. Define pressure.[1]
  2. SI unit of dynamic viscosity?[1]
  3. Continuity relation for incompressible flow?[1]
  4. Bernoulli relation?[1]
  5. Surface tension unit?[1]
  6. Pressure rise at depth h?[1]
  7. Stokes drag?[1]
  8. Excess pressure in a liquid drop?[1]

Section B · 2-mark questions — 6 × 2 = 12 marks

  1. Water at 3m depth, ρ=1000,g=10: gauge pressure?[2]
  2. If pipe area halves, incompressible speed?[2]
  3. Under Bernoulli assumptions, faster horizontal flow means pressure?[2]
  4. Force from p=5000 Pa over area .02 m²?[2]
  5. Drop S=.06 N/m,r=.003 m: excess pressure?[2]
  6. Why is a soap bubble pressure difference twice a drop's?[2]

Section C · 3-mark questions — 4 × 3 = 12 marks

  1. Pipe area 4 cm² at 2 m/s narrows to 1 cm². New speed?[3]
  2. Horizontal water speeds 2 and 6 m/s, ρ=1000. Find p₁−p₂.[3]
  3. Stokes drag for r=.5 mm,η=1 Pa s,v=.02 m/s?[3]
  4. Explain why pressure rises with depth.[3]

Section D · 4-mark questions — 2 × 4 = 8 marks

  1. Areas 6 cm² and 2 cm²; inlet speed 1.5 m/s. Find outlet speed.[4]
  2. Soap bubble r=2mm,S=.03N/m: excess pressure?[4]
