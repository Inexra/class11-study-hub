Chapter 3: Motion in a Plane

Original chapter-specific practice paper

Maximum Marks: 40Suggested time: 60 minutes
Instructions: Answer all 20 questions. Marks are shown in brackets. Show calculation steps and units. Original practice questions, not an official examination paper.

Section A · 1-mark questions — 8 × 1 = 8 marks

  1. Is displacement scalar or vector?[1]
  2. x-component at angle θ?[1]
  3. Horizontal acceleration of an ideal projectile?[1]
  4. Direction of centripetal acceleration?[1]
  5. Magnitude of perpendicular components 3 and 4?[1]
  6. Relation between v and ω?[1]
  7. Ideal maximum-range angle at equal heights?[1]
  8. Vertical velocity at projectile peak?[1]

Section B · 2-mark questions — 6 × 2 = 12 marks

  1. Resolve 10 N at 60°.[2]
  2. Displacement after a full circular lap?[2]
  3. v=12 m/s, r=6 m: a_c?[2]
  4. Horizontal launch from 20 m, g=10: flight time?[2]
  5. Perpendicular vectors 6 and 8: resultant?[2]
  6. 120 rpm in rad/s?[2]

Section C · 3-mark questions — 4 × 3 = 12 marks

  1. Projectile at 20 m/s, 30°, g=10: T,H?[3]
  2. Forces 8 N east and 6 N north: resultant?[3]
  3. Cyclist at 10 m/s, radius 25 m: a_c?[3]
  4. 25 m/s at 37°, sin=.6, cos=.8, g=10: time/range?[3]

Section D · 4-mark questions — 2 × 4 = 8 marks

  1. Projectile 20 m/s at 30°: time, height, range?[4]
  2. 5 kg at 4 m/s in radius 2 m: inward force and work?[4]

End of paper — review signs, units and working.
