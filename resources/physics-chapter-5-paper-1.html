Original chapter-specific practice paper

Instructions: Answer all 20 questions. Marks are shown in brackets. Show calculation steps and units. Original practice questions, not an official examination paper.

Section A · 1-mark questions — 8 × 1 = 8 marks

  1. When is work zero with displacement?[1]
  2. Kinetic energy?[1]
  3. SI unit of power?[1]
  4. Work-energy theorem?[1]
  5. Spring energy?[1]
  6. What is conserved in an isolated elastic collision?[1]

  8. Work by centripetal force in uniform circular motion?[1]

Section B · 2-mark questions — 6 × 2 = 12 marks

  1. 10 N over 4 m along force: work?[2]
  2. 3 kg at 4 m/s: K?[2]
  3. 100 W for 20 s: energy?[2]
  4. 200 N perpendicular to 5 m displacement: work?[2]
  5. k=200 N/m, spring stretched .1 m: energy?[2]
  6. 1 kg cart at 5 m/s sticks to stationary 4 kg: speed?[2]

Section C · 3-mark questions — 4 × 3 = 12 marks

  1. 2 kg speeds from 2 to 6 m/s: net work?[3]
  2. 5 kg falls 3 m, g=10: final speed?[3]
  3. F=4x N from x=0 to 3 m: work?[3]
  4. 1000 kg car at 20 m/s stops in 50 m: W and force magnitude?[3]

Section D · 4-mark questions — 2 × 4 = 8 marks

  1. 2 kg drops 5 m frictionlessly, g=10: speed; with 20 J loss?[4]
  2. 1 kg cart at 8 m/s sticks to 3 kg stationary: speed and energy loss?[4]
